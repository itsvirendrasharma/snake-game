❓ How to Play

🎮 Game Objective

Control the snake to eat food (red squares) and grow longer. Avoid hitting your own body!

🕹️ Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → Arrow keys or on-screen buttons
  • SPACE Pause/Resume game
  • ENTER Start new game

📊 Scoring

  • Each food eaten = +10 points
  • Your high score is saved automatically
  • Speed increases as you grow longer

🎯 Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: Snake wraps around walls (no wall collision)
  • Medium: Hitting walls ends the game
  • Hard: Faster starting speed + wall collision

💡 Tips & Tricks

  • Plan your moves ahead to avoid trapping yourself
  • In Easy mode, use wall wrapping strategically
  • Practice makes perfect - keep playing!
  • Adjust game speed in settings for your comfort
  • Try different themes for a fresh experience

⚙️ Settings

Customize your experience:

  • Themes: 7 unique themes to choose from
  • Speed: Adjust game speed from 1 (slowest) to 10 (fastest)
  • Level: Easy (wall wrap), Medium, or Hard
